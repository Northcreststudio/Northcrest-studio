How it works

From wish list to spring build

  1. 1

    Initial consultation Free

    About an hour at your home. We walk the garden together and discuss your wish list and approximate budget.

  2. 2

    Concept design

    A to-scale architectural drawing with plants, structures and precise measurements. We discuss materials, colours and plants, and give you a rough estimate.

    “The best time to consult with us is in the winter months,” so materials are ready to build.

  3. 3

    Final garden design

    Presented at your home with a detailed quote. Design costs $800–$1,500 depending on lot size, and is refunded if you go ahead with construction.

  4. 4

    Construction & build

    Usually begins in spring, when the ground is workable.

Nodepositsever

Our company policy

We never ask for a deposit.

It has always been our company policy to not ask for deposits from any of our landscaping customers. Often, a major area of stress for homeowners can be when they pay out a large deposit to a company for their landscaping. The landscaper may not always show up at the agreed time to start the landscaping project. This delay can leave the customer waiting and worrying about the money they have already paid out.

When it comes to deposits, we believe that professional companies, that use quality materials and perform quality workmanship, should not have to take a deposit.

About Ronald

Dutch horticulture, Calgary gardens.

Ronald grew up in Holland and brings Dutch and European horticulture to Calgary gardens, with over 20 years of certified horticultural experience in Europe and Canada. Every summer he works with Dutch horticulture students.

European Garden Design creates beautiful gardens and outdoor living spaces in a variety of personal styles. More than just gardens, we create an outdoor extension to your home. We pay great attention to detail which adds beauty and value to your property.

Using traditional construction techniques and incorporating environmentally integrated designs, European Garden Design combines elements of horticultural expertise and architectural features to enhance your surrounding local environment. We maximize your personal preferences in developing your property's landscape. Our seasoned and highly skilled staff will implement and interpret your wishes to its greatest effect.
