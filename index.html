Questions

How long does it take?

A Starter Site is live in about 2 weeks. For bigger sites, we give you a timeline on the free call.

Do I own my site?

If you buy your site, yes. The site, your logo files, your Google Business Profile, and the text and photos are all yours.

If you ever want to leave our hosting, we hand over a full copy of your site for free.

Your domain is always registered in your name. We set it up for you, and the yearly fee is included while you're on Care or Rent-a-Site.

What happens after the 12 months on Rent-a-Site?

It continues month to month. If you cancel, the site comes down, and you keep your domain, your logo and your Google profile.

What do I need to provide?

Photos of your work, your basic business info (services, service area, hours and contact details), and logins for your domain and Google profile if you already have them.

We write the website text for you.

Can you fix my current site?

Yes. We either improve it or rebuild it, whichever is faster and better value, and we tell you which on the free call.
