FAQ

Straight answers.

How much does a website cost for a trades business in Calgary?

With Northcrest, you can pay once from $999 and own your site, or pay monthly from $129. Most Calgary businesses budget $2,500 to $5,000 with a typical web designer, based on 2026 Calgary pricing guides. All our prices are in CAD, plus GST.

Which trades do you build websites for?

Calgary trades, including roofers, landscapers, plumbers, electricians, renovators and framers. Our first client is Hermes Framing.

What areas do you serve?

Calgary, Airdrie, Cochrane, Chestermere and Okotoks. We’re based in Calgary, Alberta.

Do you set up Google Business Profiles and reviews?

Yes. Growth sites include Google profile setup and a reviews section. On its own, a Google Profile Tune-Up is $149 and a full Pro Setup is $399. The Review Starter Kit ($249) adds QR review cards for your truck and counter, plus a review-request text to send after every job.

I get all my work from word of mouth. Why do I need a website?

Because the people you’re referred to still Google you before they call. If they find nothing, or an old site, some of them call the next name on the list.

I’m too busy for this.

That’s why we write everything. You send photos of your work and answer a few questions on a 20-minute call. We handle the rest.

I don’t want another subscription.

Then pay once and own it, from $999. Care is $79 a month if you want us to host it and make your edits.

How do I know you’re legit?

Look at hermesframing.ca (opens in a new tab), our first client’s site, and call or text us at (587) 583-8450.

How fast can my site be live?

Starter and Growth sites go live in 1 to 7 business days once we have your photos and details. Premium, which includes a custom logo, takes up to 10. If we miss that, your first month is free.

What does “see it before you pay” mean?

We design your homepage first, at no cost. If you don't love it, you walk away and owe nothing. If you do, we build the rest of the site.

What is the Founding Client offer?

Our first 10 Calgary clients get one of two things. On a monthly plan: $0 setup, and your monthly price is locked for as long as you stay, even if our prices go up later. If you buy a site from $1,999: Care is locked at $79 a month for as long as you keep it. 9 of 10 spots are left, shared between both.

Who will actually work on my site?

Every project is led by our founder, Thomas Martinez, who reviews your site personally before launch. Trusted specialists help with copywriting, photography and ads when your project needs them.

Can I cancel?

Monthly plans have a 12-month minimum, because the design and build work happens upfront. After that they go month to month, and you can cancel with 30 days' notice.

Can I own my website?

Yes. Pay once from $999 and it's yours. Your logo, Google profile and photos are always yours too. If you ever leave our hosting, we hand over a full copy of your site for free.

On a monthly plan, you can buy your site any time after 12 months for the cost of three months of your plan. If you cancel without buying it, the site comes down, and you keep your domain, your logo and your Google profile.

Do I own my domain?

Yes. Your domain is always registered in your name. We set it up for you, and the yearly fee is included while you're on a monthly plan.

What do I need to provide?

Photos of your work, your basic business info (services, service area, hours and contact details), and logins for your domain and Google profile if you already have them. We write the website text for you.

My nephew made my site. Can you fix it?

Yes. We’ll tell you honestly if improving it beats rebuilding. Website Rescue ($999) keeps your site and fixes speed, mobile, the homepage, click-to-call and the quote form.

How do I pay?

By card or Interac e-Transfer to hello@northcreststudio.ca. Monthly plans are billed automatically. All prices are in CAD, plus GST.

Do you pay for referrals?

Yes. When someone you refer signs up, you get $100 or a free month. Sign shops, print shops, trade suppliers and bookkeepers can partner with us for $100 per signed client.
