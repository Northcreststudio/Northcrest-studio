Privacy policy

Last updated September 30, 2026

Who we are

Northcrest Studio is a web design studio in Calgary, Alberta. You can reach us at hello@northcreststudio.ca or (587) 583-8450.

What the contact form collects

When you send the form on our website, we receive what you type in:

  • Your name and phone number
  • Your email address, if you add it
  • Your type of business
  • Whether you have a website, and its link if you add it
  • What you're interested in, and your message

Why we collect it

We use this information only to reply to you about your enquiry, for example to call or text you back, answer your questions or send you a quote.

What we never do

We never sell your information, and we never share it with anyone for their marketing.

Where it goes

Form messages are delivered to us through Netlify, the company that hosts our website.

Asking us to delete it

You can ask us to delete your information at any time. Email hello@northcreststudio.ca with the name and phone number you used, and we'll delete it and confirm by email.

Questions

If you have any questions about this policy, email hello@northcreststudio.ca.
