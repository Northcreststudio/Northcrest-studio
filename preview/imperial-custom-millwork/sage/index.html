What we do

No catalogue sizes. No standard boxes.

Mike designs, builds and installs each piece to suit the room, the house and the way you live in it. If you don't see your project here, just ask.

  1. 01

    Custom kitchens & cabinetry

    Cabinetry made for your layout, set level and square and fitted tight to walls and ceilings, with hardware and fillers finished clean.

  2. 02

    Built-ins & storage

    Pantries, shelving, benches, mudroom lockers and entertainment walls, built to fit the space instead of bought off the shelf.

  3. 03

    Trim, doors & mouldings

    Baseboards, casing and crown moulding with tight mitres and clean corners, plus interior doors hung with even reveals.

  4. 04

    Stairs & feature walls

    Stair treads, risers, skirt boards and railings, and wainscoting, board and batten or panel moulding for a plain wall.

About us

Meet Mike & Cathy

Imperial Custom Millwork Ltd. is owned and run by Mike and Cathy Robertson. Mike is a journeyman finishing carpenter, and the company has been in business since 2003. After moving from Alberta to British Columbia, the business now works out of Kelowna in the Okanagan.

What is custom millwork?

The woodwork that finishes a home: cabinetry, built-ins, trim, stairs and panelling, made for your space instead of bought off the shelf.

What affects the cost of a custom piece?

Size, material, finish and the amount of detail, plus install conditions like walls that aren't square. A walkthrough gives you an exact number.

How long does a project take?

A built-in is much quicker than a full kitchen. You'll get a timeline with your quote.
