FAQ

Common questions

What is custom millwork?

Millwork is the woodwork that finishes a home: cabinetry, built-ins, trim, stairs and panelling. Custom millwork is made for your space instead of bought off the shelf, so it fits the room exactly and matches the rest of the house.

What affects the cost of a custom piece?

Size, the wood or material, the finish, and the amount of detail (doors, drawers, mouldings, hardware). Install conditions matter too, such as walls that aren't level or square. A walkthrough gives you an exact number.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the size of the job. A built-in or vanity is much quicker than a full kitchen. You'll get a timeline with your quote.

Can you match existing trim or cabinetry?

Custom work can usually be made to match the profiles, wood and finish already in your home, so new pieces blend in.

Do I need a permit?

Cabinetry, built-ins and trim generally don't need a building permit on their own. Permits usually come into play when walls, structure, plumbing or electrical change as part of a wider renovation.
