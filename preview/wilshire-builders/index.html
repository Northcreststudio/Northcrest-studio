Building in Calgary: common questions

Who is Wilshire Builders?

Wilshire Builders is a residential building and renovation company in Calgary, Alberta. It was established in 2014 and is owner-operated by Ben Clark. Call (403) 563-9443 or email wilshirebuilders.ben@hotmail.com.

What drives the cost of building or renovating a home in Calgary?

Size and layout, the foundation type, the condition and access of the lot, the finishes you choose, and whether walls or structure need to change. A quote after a site visit is the only reliable number.

What is an ICF foundation, and why use one?

ICF stands for insulated concrete forms. Hollow foam blocks are stacked into the shape of the foundation walls and filled with concrete. The foam stays in place, so the walls are insulated on both sides, which helps keep basements warmer and drier through Calgary winters.

Do I need a building permit in Calgary?

New homes, additions, basement development and most structural changes in Calgary need a building permit from the City of Calgary. Cosmetic work such as painting or replacing cabinets usually does not.

Is a new home in Alberta covered by a warranty?

Yes. Under Alberta's New Home Buyer Protection Act, new homes must carry third-party warranty coverage: 1 year on labour and materials, 2 years on delivery and distribution systems, 5 years on the building envelope and 10 years on the structure.
